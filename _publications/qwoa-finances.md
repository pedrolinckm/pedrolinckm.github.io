title: Quantum walk mixers for fixed-cardinality QAOA are limited by repeated eigenvalues, not by edge density
collection: publications
category: inpreparation
permalink: /publication/qwoa-finances
excerpt: 'Under conditions we make explicit, constraint-preserving QAOA mixers are continuous-time quantum walks on the feasible set, so choosing a mixer amounts to choosing a graph. For fixed-cardinality portfolio selection we compare every feasible graph invariant under permutations of the assets: the unions of classes of the permutation-invariant scheme, from the Johnson graph $$J(n,k)$$ of the $$XY$$ mixer to the complete graph of the Grover mixer. Performance does not grow with the edge density. Members with repeated eigenvalues lose 8% to 44% of the probability of sampling the lowest 5% of costs relative to $$J(n,k)$$, and the complete graph, with a single nontrivial eigenvalue, about two thirds. $$J(n,k)$$, the sparsest member and the only two-local one, is within 3% of the best after one layer and leads the mixers tested from two layers on.'
