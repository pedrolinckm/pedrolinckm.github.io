title: A dynamical-systems criterion for optical bistability in energy-transfer upconversion
collection: publications
category: inpreparation
permalink: /publication/optical-bistability
excerpt: 'Optical bistability in rare-earth upconversion has been reported material by material, but a general condition on the rate constants that decides whether a given phosphor is monostable or bistable has been lacking. We analyze the standard Yb→Er energy-transfer rate equations as a dynamical system, retaining the ground-state-depletion feedback. The fixed-point structure is governed by a cubic whose fold (saddle-node) bifurcations set the boundary of the bistable region. Using the cubic discriminant, we show instead that the boundary reduces to a triple-root (cusp) condition whose supremum over the remaining rates gives an exact, scale-invariant bound: bistability requires the sensitizer-to-activator transfer rate to exceed the emitting-level decay rate by a factor of at least 79.6, approached only as the sensitizer decay vanishes and the remaining transfer rates diverge together.'
